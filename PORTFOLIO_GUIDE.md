## How I Connect the Three

1. **Understand the goal** — clarify the business need and what success means.
2. **Create visibility** — use clear requirements, progress tracking, and relevant metrics.
3. **Move toward a decision** — communicate risks and insights, align stakeholders, and define the next action.
